Cabinet to CAM

Configure cabinets and common hardware, inspect the individual parts, then nest by material and thickness. Download the selected sheet as STEP in Nesting, or all sheets with stock dimensions and setup instructions in Documents.

STEP contains separate named solid parts with their holes and rebates. X/Y start at the sheet lower-left; Z0 is stock top. Set stock, cutters, machining strategies, feeds, tabs and your machine post in Fusion or another CAM application.

Glass doors have two full-height stiles and two rails, rear glass rebates and paired frame-dowel bores. Edge holes need edge drilling after cutout. Buy glass separately from the cut list and confirm its fit after assembling the frame.

Autosave stays in this browser. Save project JSON to keep a portable backup. Earlier machine settings are retained in project files for compatibility; machining faces are always oriented up for STEP export.
